# Approved Brand and Content Assets

Only place final, reusable client assets in the shared Drive folder:

- Approved logo files and any supplied brand guide
- Original team, advisor, programme, and event photographs
- Approved video links and reusable biographies or programme copy
- Final legal copy supplied or approved by client counsel
- Approved Figma, LMS course, asset-library, or store-listing links

Keep filenames clear and retain original high-resolution images. Include usage
permissions or credits where relevant.

Do not upload source code, passwords, API keys, CMS backups, raw form
submissions, analytics exports, deployment settings, or unrelated staff files.
