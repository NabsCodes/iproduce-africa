## Operating boundary

Tobi coordinates routine website CMS work. The website team handles code,
hosting, deployments, forms, DNS, integrations, layout, and new functionality.

The LMS and mobile app are separate products managed by their respective
development teams. The website CMS only manages approved public links and
promotional content that points to them.

GitHub and Vercel remain developer-managed under the current arrangement. Any
future technical transfer must be agreed separately in writing.

## Before closeout

- Client counsel reviews or replaces the working legal-page copy.
- Genuine Academy event dates and times are confirmed before publishing.
- Temporary Zoho passwords are changed and recovery/2FA is confirmed.
- Approved LMS links and mobile-app messaging are supplied by the relevant
  product teams before website publication.

Detailed editing instructions are in the CMS Editor Guide. Account ownership
and remaining actions are maintained in the restricted access workbook.
