# Support and Next Steps

Use the restricted **Access and Open Items Register** as the live tracker. Do
not maintain a second detailed action table here.

## Requesting website support

Send:

1. the affected page URL or CMS record name;
2. what you expected and what happened instead;
3. a screenshot or short recording where useful; and
4. whether the request is a correction, access problem, or new change.

Routine editorial updates belong in the CMS. New pages, layout changes,
integrations, forms, hosting/DNS work, or new functionality may require a
separate scope and estimate.

LMS issues go to the separate LMS team. Mobile-app issues go to the separate
mobile-app team. The website team handles the public website, Sanity CMS, and
the approved links or promotional content displayed there.
